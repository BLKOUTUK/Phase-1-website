# BLKOUT Community Credits System
**Liberation Economics: Rewarding Contribution, Building Community**

**Philosophy**: "From each according to ability, to each according to contribution - with dignity, not extraction."

---

## Why Community Credits > Cash Stipends

### Problems with Cash Stipends

❌ **Budget Dependent**: Requires £10k+/year (may not be sustainable)
❌ **Creates Hierarchy**: Paid vs. unpaid members (division)
❌ **Tax Complexity**: Income reporting, HMRC compliance
❌ **Limited Pool**: Can only reward those in formal volunteer roles
❌ **Extractive Framing**: Feels like "compensation for labor"

### Benefits of Community Credits

✅ **Budget Flexible**: Can start with zero budget, scale as funds allow
✅ **Inclusive**: Rewards all contributions (not just formal volunteers)
✅ **Community Building**: Merch creates visible community identity
✅ **Values Aligned**: Tree planting, Kwanda donations = liberation economics
✅ **Gamification**: Points feel like achievement, not transaction
✅ **Tax Simple**: Credits for merch = gifts, not taxable income
✅ **Sustainable**: Merch cost controlled, scales with community size
✅ **Circular Economy**: Credits encourage ongoing participation

## Community Credits System Architecture

### How It Works

```
Community Contribution → Points Earned → Credits Accumulated → Rewards Redeemed

Example:
Sarah welcomes 10 new members (100 points) +
Facilitates 5 discussions (50 points) +
Shares resource that helps 20 people (40 points) =
190 points = 19 credits

Redeem:
- BLKOUT T-shirt (15 credits)
- Plant 4 trees (4 credits)
- Keep 0 credits for next month
```

**Key Principle**: 10 points = 1 credit (easy mental math)

## Credits Economy Management

### Initial Pricing Strategy

**Cost per Credit** (for budgeting):
- **Target**: 1 credit = £1-2 real cost to BLKOUT
- **T-shirt**: 25 credits = £25-30 cost (bulk pricing)
- **Tree planting**: 1 credit = £1 via Ecologi
- **Kwanda credit**: 1 credit = £1 direct transfer
- **High-value items**: Lower credit cost (e.g., conference ticket 200 credits but £300 value = investment in leadership)

**Why This Works**:
- Members earn credits through contribution (time/value given)
- BLKOUT converts credits to rewards (cost controlled)
- High earners get high-value rewards (fair exchange)
- Low-cost items (stickers, trees) maximize accessibility

## Preventing Gaming & Abuse

### Anti-Gaming Mechanisms

**1. Frequency Caps**:
- Can't spam 100 low-quality posts for points
- Quality > Quantity enforced

**2. Peer Verification**:
- High-value actions (facilitated discussion, created resource) require peer confirmation
- 3+ members must validate: "This was helpful/valuable"

**3. Moderator Review**:
- Suspicious patterns flagged (e.g., 500 points in one day)
- Stewards can audit and adjust points if gaming detected

**4. Diminishing Returns**:
- First 10 posts/week = 5 points each
- Posts 11-20 = 3 points each
- Posts 21+ = 1 point each
- Encourages diverse contributions, not spam

**5. Negative Points** (Guideline Violations):
- Minor violation: -25 points
- Major violation: -100 points + suspension
- Repeated violations: Points reset to 0

**6. Expiration** (Optional, Use Carefully):
- Credits don't expire (fair, no pressure)
- BUT: Inactive members (6+ months no activity) → points freeze (can reactivate)

## Gamification Done Right

### Healthy Gamification (Encourage)

✅ **Progress Milestones**: "You've earned 100 credits! Halfway to a hoodie!"
✅ **Community Celebrations**: "Our community planted 1,000 trees together! 🌳"
✅ **Skill Badges**: "Resource Creator" badge for contributing guides
✅ **Unlocks**: Active Member status unlocks higher-value redemptions
✅ **Streaks** (Optional): "Welcomed new members 7 days in a row! +bonus"

### Unhealthy Gamification (Avoid)

❌ **Leaderboard Obsession**: Don't make it too competitive (creates hierarchy)
❌ **FOMO Pressure**: Don't expire credits too soon (creates anxiety)
❌ **Grind Culture**: Don't require excessive points for basic rewards
❌ **Pay-to-Win**: Can't buy credits with money (earned only through contribution)
❌ **Shame**: Don't publicly rank "least active" members

**Balance**: Celebrate contribution, don't create competition that divides

## FAQ: Community Credits

**Q: Can I buy credits with money?**
A: No. Credits are earned only through contribution. This keeps the system values-aligned (contribution, not transaction).

**Q: Do credits expire?**
A: No. Your credits never expire. Take your time, save up for what you want.

**Q: Can I transfer credits to another member?**
A: Not initially (prevents secondary market). Future: Maybe gift credits for birthdays/special occasions.

**Q: What if I can't afford the redemption shipping cost?**
A: UK shipping is free. International members: We cover shipping or offer digital/local alternatives.

**Q: Can I donate my credits?**
A: Yes! Redeem for liberation economics (trees, Kwanda for others, community meals, etc.)

**Q: How are points verified?**
A: Most actions tracked automatically (n8n). High-value actions require peer verification (3+ members confirm).

**Q: What if I disagree with points awarded/deducted?**
A: Appeal process: Contact Steward → Review evidence → Adjust if error → Transparent decision.

**Q: Is the leaderboard mandatory?**
A: No. Community votes on whether to display leaderboard. Can opt-out of appearing publicly.

**Q: Can I see how my credits help the community?**

**Q: What happens to unclaimed credits if someone leaves?**
