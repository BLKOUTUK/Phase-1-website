## CONFLICTS OF INTEREST ASSESSMENT

### Identified Conflict: NORTHANTS RAINBOW COLLECTIVE CIC

**Nature of Conflict:**
Two BLKOUT founding members—**Olamide Adesanya** and **Cardew Jackson-Cole**—serve together as co-directors of **NORTHANTS RAINBOW COLLECTIVE CIC** (Company No. 15134336), both appointed 13 August 2023.

**Potential Impact:**
If BLKOUT CBS were to enter into any transactions, partnerships, or funding relationships with NORTHANTS RAINBOW COLLECTIVE CIC, both Olamide and Cardew would have a direct personal interest in the outcome.

**Mitigation Strategy:**

1. **Conflict of Interest Register**: BLKOUT will maintain a formal conflict of interest register documenting this shared directorship. This register will be reviewed at every board meeting.

2. **Recusal Policy**: Should any decision arise involving NORTHANTS RAINBOW COLLECTIVE CIC (including but not limited to: partnerships, grant decisions, service contracts, shared projects), both Olamide Adesanya and Cardew Jackson-Cole will:
   - Declare their interest at the start of the relevant meeting
   - Leave the room during discussion and voting
   - Not participate in any decision-making regarding the matter
   - Have their recusal recorded in meeting minutes

3. **Transparency**: All BLKOUT members will be informed of this conflict and the recusal procedure via our governance documentation and member handbook.

**Assessment**: This conflict is **manageable** and does **not disqualify** either member from BLKOUT founding membership, provided the above mitigation measures are consistently applied.

### Other Affiliations Review

**Robert Berkeley** holds 4 current directorships/trusteeships in the charitable sector (Black Boy Joy Club CIC, Doc Society Charitable Trust, Riverside Trust, Stanley Arts). None of these organizations overlap with other founding members' affiliations, and none present conflicts with BLKOUT's mission or operations.

**Reuben Silungwe** serves as a trustee of **KALEIDOSCOPE TRUST**, an LGBTQ+ human rights charity. While there is thematic alignment with BLKOUT's mission (both address LGBTQ+ and intersectional issues), there is no organizational conflict. KALEIDOSCOPE TRUST operates internationally focusing on legal/policy advocacy, whereas BLKOUT focuses on UK community-led cultural infrastructure. No transactional conflict exists.

**Conclusion**: Only one material conflict identified (Olamide/Cardew CIC co-directorship), which is documented and mitigated through formal recusal procedures.
