## What You Need to Declare

The FCA requires disclosure of ALL organizational affiliations within the last 3 years, including:

✅ **Companies** (Ltd, PLC, CIC)
- Director, Secretary, or Member positions

✅ **Charities**
- Trustee or officer positions

✅ **Cooperatives & Societies** (Including other Community Benefit Societies)
- Committee member, officer, or member positions

✅ **Partnerships**
- Partner in any LLP or traditional partnership

✅ **Sole Traders**
- Trading under your own name or business name

✅ **Unincorporated Associations**
- Committee member or officer in community groups, clubs, etc.
