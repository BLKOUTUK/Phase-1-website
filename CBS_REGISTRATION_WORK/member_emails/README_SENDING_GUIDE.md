**Use BCC for privacy:**
1. Copy email content from `.md` file
2. Paste into email client (Gmail, Outlook, etc.)
3. Send individually to each member (NOT group email - personalized content differs)
4. Subject: **BLKOUT CBS Registration - Quick Confirmation Needed (FCA Requirement)**
5. Mark as **High Priority**

**Why not group email?**
- Each email contains personalized findings specific to that member
- Some members have no affiliations, others have multiple

### Sending Schedule

**All emails should be sent at the same time** to ensure fairness and consistent deadline.

**Recommended timing:**
- **Best**: Monday-Wednesday morning (9am-11am) - highest email open rates
- **Avoid**: Friday afternoon or weekends - lower response rates

## Communication Tips

### Tone

- **Urgent but not alarming**: "FCA deadline approaching" not "CRITICAL EMERGENCY"
- **Appreciative**: Thank them for their time
- **Clear**: "This takes 2 minutes" sets expectations

### If Members Ask Questions

**"Why does FCA need this?"**
- Standard due diligence for CBS registration
- Ensures good governance and no undisclosed conflicts
- Similar to what's required for company directors

**"What if I'm not sure if something needs to be disclosed?"**
- General rule: Any formal role with legal responsibilities (director, trustee, secretary, partner)
- Employment, casual memberships, artistic collaborations: NO
- When in doubt, include it - we can assess relevance

**"What happens if I have a conflict of interest?"**
- Not a problem! Just needs to be documented
- You'll recuse yourself from relevant decisions
- Standard governance practice, doesn't disqualify you

**"Can I update this later if I remember something?"**
- Yes, but ideally get it complete now for FCA deadline
- Changes after submission may require amended FCA letter

## Troubleshooting

### "I can't access the Google Form"

- Check if form is set to "Anyone with link can respond"
- Try different browser or incognito mode
- Offer to take response via email or phone instead

### "The email is too long, I don't have time to read it"

- Direct them to the Google Form link
- Or: Summarize over phone - "Just need you to confirm: we found X affiliations for you, is that correct?"

### "I don't understand what needs to be disclosed"

- Send them this simple rule: "Any board position where you're legally responsible (director, trustee, company secretary)"
- Give examples: YES - company director, charity trustee, co-op board member | NO - employee, consultant, artistic collaborator

### "One member is completely unresponsive"

- Try all channels: Email, phone, text, WhatsApp, Signal
- Ask other founding members to reach out
- Last resort: Phone call explaining you cannot proceed without their response (FCA requirement is 100% disclosure)
