- **Focus**: Early identification, intervention, positive mental health programs
- **BLKOUT fit**: ⚠️ Weak - not focused on children/young people specifically

**Must have**:
- ✅ UK-registered charity (❓ **IS BLKOUT REGISTERED AS CHARITY?**)

## 2. WCIT Charity (Worshipful Company of Information Technologists)

### Overview
- **Grant size**: Up to £15,000 (exceptional cases higher)
- **Deadlines**: 10 February 2025 or 7 May 2025
- **Success rate**: **Only 2%** (very competitive!)
- **Purpose**: Use tech for impact through digital inclusion, education, charitable/public engagement

### Eligibility
- ✅ UK registered charities
- ✅ Educational establishments
- ✅ Not-for-profit organisations (CICs, community interest companies)
- ✅ **ALL projects must use information technology**

### What They Fund
**More likely to fund**:
- ✅ Development of innovative new services/solutions
- ✅ Training, apps, analytics, AI, robotics
- ✅ Accessibility features/hardware
- ✅ Projects where they are material or sole funder
- ✅ Sole funder of IT component of larger project
- ✅ Organisations that could benefit from pro bono IT support

**Less likely to fund**:
- ❌ Hardware refresh (laptop/desktop replacement)
- ❌ Off-the-shelf software packages
- ❌ Websites
- ❌ Network/telephony costs
- ❌ Cloud migration
- ❌ Large national charities with significant reserves
- ❌ Projects where contribution/impact is very small

### BLKOUT Angle

**The Pitch**:
"BLKOUTHUB uses Heartbeat.chat platform to create digital community infrastructure for QTIPOC individuals. We're seeking funding to develop custom features: member match-up algorithm (skills-based pairing), analytics dashboard (community health metrics), and accessibility improvements (screen reader optimization, translation features)."

**Funding request** (£15k):
- Custom member match-up algorithm development (£5k)
- Community analytics dashboard (liberation metrics) (£5k)
- Accessibility features (£3k)
- Integration development (n8n automation) (£2k)

**Strengths**:
- ✅ Tech platform (fits their mandate)
- ✅ Digital inclusion for underserved community
- ✅ Innovative use of tech (community organizing)
- ✅ Could benefit from pro bono IT support (they like this)

**Weaknesses**:
- ❌ 2% success rate (highly competitive)
- ❌ Small grant size (£15k max)
- ⚠️ Heartbeat is off-the-shelf platform (they're less likely to fund)
- ⚠️ Website/platform development is explicitly less likely to fund
- ⚠️ Would need to position as "custom feature development" not "platform usage"

**Likelihood**: **LOW** - 2% success rate, platform usage not their priority
