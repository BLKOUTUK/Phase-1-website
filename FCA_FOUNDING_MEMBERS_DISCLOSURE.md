## TASK 2: Public Registry Searches

### Step-by-Step Search Guide

#### A. Companies House (Directorships & Company Memberships)

**URL**: https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/

**Search Process**:
1. Click "Search the register"
2. Select "Officers" tab
3. Enter founding member's full name (try variations: first + last, middle names, initials)
4. Note: Shows current AND resigned directorships/secretaryships
5. For each result:
   - Click through to company details
   - Note company name, number, type (Ltd, PLC, CIC)
   - Note member's role (Director, Secretary)
   - Note appointment date and resignation date (if resigned)
   - Check "Filing history" for confirmation statements

**What to Record**:
```
Member: [NAME]
Company: [COMPANY NAME]
Registration: [COMPANY NUMBER]
Type: [Ltd / PLC / CIC / LLP]
Role: [Director / Secretary / Member]
Appointed: [DD/MM/YYYY]
Resigned: [DD/MM/YYYY or "Current"]
Status: [Active / Dissolved / In liquidation]
```

**Common Variations to Try**:
- Full name: "Jane Mary Smith"
- First + Last: "Jane Smith"
- With middle initial: "Jane M Smith"
- Hyphenated names: try with/without hyphen
- Previous surnames (if known)

**Red Flags**:
- Disqualified director status (automatic CBS rejection)
- Company in liquidation or dissolved (note for conflicts check)
- Multiple directorships in similar sectors (potential conflict)

---

#### B. Charity Commission Register (Trusteeships)

**URL**: https://register-of-charities.charitycommission.gov.uk/

**Search Process**:
1. Enter charity name OR member name in search
2. Note: Trustee names only shown on charity detail pages, not in main search
3. Strategy: Search for organizations member might be involved with, then check trustees list
4. Alternative: Download charity accounts (recent accounts list trustees)

**Limitations**:
- No direct "search by trustee name" function
- Must search charity-by-charity
- Trustee lists only on recent filings (last 2 years)

**What to Record**:
```
Member: [NAME]
Charity: [CHARITY NAME]
Registration: [CHARITY NUMBER]
Role: [Trustee / Secretary / Chair]
Appointed: [YEAR - often not precise]
Status: [Current / Former]
```

**Tip**: Ask member directly for charity trusteeships - registry search is incomplete

---

#### C. FCA Mutuals Register (Cooperatives & Societies)

**URL**: https://mutuals.fca.org.uk/

**Search Process**:
1. Search by "Society name" for organizations member mentioned
2. Click through to society details
3. Check "Officers" section (may require document download)
4. Note: Limited searchability by individual name

**What to Record**:
```
Member: [NAME]
Society: [SOCIETY NAME]
Registration: [FCA NUMBER]
Type: [Co-operative / Community Benefit Society]
Role: [Committee member / Secretary / Treasurer]
Appointed: [YEAR]
```

**Limitation**: Officer details often only in annual returns (not always public-facing)

---

#### D. LinkedIn & Professional Profiles (Cross-Reference)

**Use For**:
- Verifying current employment/roles
- Identifying organizations to search in registries
- Finding company names for Companies House search

**What to Look For**:
- "Board Member at [Organization]"
- "Trustee at [Charity]"
- "Director at [Company]"
- "Founder of [Enterprise]"

**Important**: LinkedIn is NOT sufficient evidence - must verify in official registries

### Master Affiliations Spreadsheet

**Instructions**: Combine member responses + registry search findings into single authoritative list.

| Founding Member | Organization Name | Legal Form | Member Role | Reg Number | Appointed | Resigned/Left | Current Status | Source | Conflict Risk |
|-----------------|-------------------|------------|-------------|------------|-----------|---------------|----------------|--------|---------------|
| [NAME] | [ORG] | Ltd/CIC/Charity/Co-op | Director/Trustee | [NUMBER] | MM/YYYY | MM/YYYY or Current | Active/Dissolved | Member/CH/CC/FCA | Low/Medium/High |
| | | | | | | | | | |
| | | | | | | | | | |

**Column Definitions**:
- **Source**: Member (self-reported), CH (Companies House), CC (Charity Commission), FCA (Mutuals Register), LinkedIn
- **Conflict Risk**:
  - **Low**: No overlap with BLKOUT activities
  - **Medium**: Similar sector but different focus (e.g., arts charity when BLKOUT does community organizing)
  - **High**: Direct overlap (e.g., another QTIPOC community organization, competing cooperative)

### Conflicts of Interest Assessment

**For each "Medium" or "High" risk affiliation, document**:
```
Member: [NAME]
Organization: [ORG NAME]
Nature of potential conflict: [Describe overlap with BLKOUT's activities]
Mitigation: [How conflict will be managed]
  Examples:
  - Member will recuse from BLKOUT decisions affecting [ORG]
  - Organizations operate in different geographic areas
  - Organizations serve different communities
  - Member's role is non-decision-making (e.g., ordinary member, not director)
```

**Acceptable Conflicts** (disclose but don't require mitigation):
- Trade union membership
- Professional body membership
- Community groups without commercial overlap
- Arts/cultural organizations
- Educational institutions

**Unacceptable Conflicts** (may block CBS registration):
- Director of competing cooperative in same sector
- Financial interest in supplier to BLKOUT
- Family member relationship with BLKOUT contractor
- Undisclosed business relationship with BLKOUT

## Red Flags & How to Handle

### Scenario 1: Member is Disqualified Director
**Red Flag**: Companies House shows member is disqualified from acting as company director

**Impact**: May block CBS registration - FCA unlikely to approve

**Action**:
1. Verify finding (double-check Companies House entry)
2. Contact member immediately: confirm disqualification status and dates
3. If confirmed: member must resign as founding member OR seek legal advice about FCA exemption
4. Notify FCA immediately with plan to address (member resignation or exemption application)

---

### Scenario 2: Undisclosed Material Conflict
**Red Flag**: Registry search reveals directorship in competing QTIPOC organization that member didn't mention

**Impact**: Raises integrity concerns with FCA

**Action**:
1. Contact member: "I found X in Companies House - can you explain?"
2. Possible explanations:
   - Member forgot (understandable if dormant/resigned role)
   - Member didn't consider it relevant (education needed)
   - Registry outdated (member resigned but not yet reflected)
3. Include in disclosure with note: "Initially unreported, identified through registry search, member confirms [status]"
4. Assess conflict: can it be managed or is it disqualifying?

---

### Scenario 3: Member Doesn't Respond by Deadline
**Red Flag**: Member hasn't responded by Day 3 despite reminders

**Action**:
1. Day 3 final attempt: phone call + text + email: "FCA deadline in 4 days, need your response TODAY"
2. If still no response: proceed with registry search findings only
3. Disclosure note: "[Member name] affiliations based on public registry searches conducted [date]. Member contacted [dates] but did not provide self-declaration. We have disclosed all affiliations identified through diligent registry searches."
4. Risk: If member has affiliations not in public registries (e.g., recent trusteeships), they'll be missed
5. Mitigation: Document outreach attempts, proceed with available information

---

### Scenario 4: Complex Conflict Requiring Legal Advice
**Red Flag**: Member is director of organization that contracts with BLKOUT OR family member relationship with supplier

**Impact**: Serious conflict, may require governance changes

**Action**:
1. Seek legal advice immediately (co-op solicitor, FCA guidance)
2. Options:
   - Member recuses from relevant decisions (documented in governance)
   - Member resigns directorship from conflicting organization
   - Member resigns as BLKOUT founding member
   - BLKOUT changes supplier/contractor to remove conflict
3. Document in FCA response: "Legal advice sought from [solicitor], mitigation plan: [X]"

---

### Scenario 5: Member Has 10+ Affiliations
**Red Flag**: Member is director of multiple companies, trustee of several charities

**Impact**: Not inherently problematic, but raises questions about time commitment and conflicts

**Action**:
1. List all affiliations (don't cherry-pick)
2. Assess each for conflicts
3. FCA response note: "[Member] has extensive organizational experience including [number] current roles. We have assessed each for potential conflicts with BLKOUT's activities and found [X/no] material conflicts. [Member] has confirmed capacity to fulfill founding member duties."
