## Second-Round Strategy: Why It Matters

**Key insight**: Funders often prefer second-round applicants because:

1. **Demonstrates commitment** (didn't give up after one try)
2. **Shows learning** (incorporated feedback from first round)
3. **Builds relationship** (funder knows your org, your work)
4. **Proves seriousness** (not just chasing money, genuinely aligned)

**How to prepare for second rounds**:

1. **First application = reconnaissance**
   - Learn funder's priorities (what do they emphasize in feedback?)
   - Understand application process (what was hard? what was easy?)
   - Build relationship (call with grants officer, attend funder events)

2. **After rejection, always**:
   - Request feedback (phone call > email)
   - Thank them for consideration
   - Ask: "Would you encourage us to reapply in future rounds?"
   - Ask: "What would strengthen our next application?"

3. **Between rounds**:
   - Implement feedback
   - Gather more evidence of impact
   - Stay in touch (share updates, invite to events)
   - Attend funder convenings if invited

4. **Second application**:
   - Reference previous application ("We applied in 2025 and appreciate your feedback")
   - Show how you've addressed concerns
   - Demonstrate growth (new evidence, expanded impact)

**Example: LGBT+ Futures Equity Fund**
- **Round 1** (Feb 2026): Apply for £10k organizational development
- **Outcome**: Learn about their priorities, get feedback
- **Between rounds**: Build organizational capacity, gather member testimonials
- **Round 2** (2027): Apply for £25k project funding, showing strengthened org

**Example: National Lottery Reaching Communities**
- **Round 1** (Mar 2026): Apply for £150k, learn their process
- **If rejected**: Request feedback, ask if can reapply
- **Between rounds**: Implement pilot project (smaller scale), gather evidence
- **Round 2** (2027): Reapply with pilot results, stronger evidence base

## Funding vs Earned Revenue

**Strategic question**: Should BLKOUT rely on grants or build earned revenue?

**Grants**:
- ✅ No debt, no repayment
- ✅ Validates mission (funder endorsement)
- ⚠️ Time-intensive applications
- ⚠️ Restricted funding (must use for stated purpose)
- ⚠️ Unsustainable long-term (constant reapplication)

**Earned revenue (cooperative memberships)**:
- ✅ Sustainable (recurring income)
- ✅ Flexible (unrestricted funds)
- ✅ Community ownership (members = stakeholders)
- ⚠️ Slow to build (takes time to reach scale)
- ⚠️ Requires value proposition (members pay for tangible benefits)

**Recommended hybrid model**:
- **Year 1-2**: Grants for infrastructure (LGBT+ Futures, National Lottery, Flexible Finance)
- **Year 2-3**: Grants + growing membership revenue
- **Year 3+**: Membership revenue primary, grants for specific projects

**Membership revenue targets**:
- 100 members × £5/month = £6,000/year
- 250 members × £10/month = £30,000/year
- 500 members × £15/month = £90,000/year

**If 500 paying members**: BLKOUT could be grant-independent.
